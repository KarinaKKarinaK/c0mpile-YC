## Worst issues
1. **Rigged-looking headline metric.** "500/500 caught" on fakes we generated. YC founders will smell it. Fix: demote to a mechanism test, labelled synthetic, never in the hero.
2. **Two customers.** Fix: operator is the user ("Finance your robots. Repay only when they work."). Lenders appear only as "capital partners".
3. **Wall of text.** Fix: keywords and numbers only; one line per tile.
4. **Toy 3D.** Fix: a real factory where a line jams, the beacon spins, revenue drops, and the loan flexes.
5. **No why-us.** Fix: team tile with the three prior builds.

## Fix these now
1. Highest impact: rewrite landing to keywords + numbers + one clear CTA "Finance a robot".
2. Easiest win: founder tile and proxies tile.
3. Existential: real users. Film 2 to 3 robot founders at the venue before 22:00.
